# Ambient sound

Menu › Sound: Ambient Sound toggle, Volume, Your Music (choose folder, volume, stop), Sleep Timer, Use Your Own Sounds…, Sound Credits.
Menu › Presets: Deep Winter, Autumn Evening, Cozy Rain, Spring Morning, Focus, own presets (Option-click to delete), Save Current as Preset….

| Mode | Beds | Calls |
|---|---|---|
| Snow | snow-night 0.9, snow-wind 0.45 | — |
| Autumn | autumn-trees 0.85, autumn-leaves 0.5 | — |
| Rain | rain-leaves 0.85, rain-window 0.5 | thunder ×3, every 70–160 s |
| Blossom | blossom-breeze 0.6, blossom-birds 0.6 | — |
| Halloween (snow) | halloween-night 0.6 | owl ×2 35–90 s, crow ×2 60–150 s |

Level = volume × (0.45 + 0.55 × intensity); Mixed modes play both at 0.7; 3 s fades on any change.
Custom sounds: `~/Library/Application Support/Snowhush/Sounds/<snow|autumn|rain|blossom|halloween>/` — any audio files there replace that mode's beds.
Preferences keys: soundOn, soundVolume, musicVolume, musicFolder, presets.
